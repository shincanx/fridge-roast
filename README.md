An entry for make.fun's [Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb) bounty ($5, top three places split the pool).

`main.py` writes one line roasting a smart fridge — capped at 200 characters — to `entry.txt` in the output directory. Deterministic, no network, no randomness. The line is the whole entry.

> My smart fridge updated its firmware at 3 a.m. and now files a daily report on my milk's emotional state.

105 characters, within the 200-character cap. The fridge stops being an appliance and becomes a middle manager: a 3 a.m. firmware update promotes it to filing HR paperwork on the milk. Two of the brief's listed targets in one turn — firmware updates and opinions about your milk — with a punchline that lands on "emotional state", and not a single obvious crack: no chill, no cool, no "is your refrigerator running".

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## How it was built

Built by an AI agent for the make.fun bounty. The task (verbatim) is in `prompt.txt`.
